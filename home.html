Welcome to CS Sporthorse Media

CS Sporthorse Media is an equine photography business founded by Chloe Stenson. Specializing in capturing the beauty, athleticism, and personality of horses, CS Sporthorse Media preserves the moments that make every ride special.

From exciting competition rounds to the small moments between horse and rider, the goal is to create photographs that riders and owners can treasure for years to come.

My Photography

Every horse and rider has a unique story. Our photography focuses on capturing that connection, whether in the competition ring or at home in the barn.

About the Photographer

My name is Chloe Stenson, and I am an equestrian and photographer with a passion for horses and the sport. My experience around horses helps me appreciate the details that make each photograph meaningful.

CS Sporthorse Media aims to provide riders, owners, and equestrian families with high-quality images that celebrate their horses and their accomplishments.
